10分钟对话练习：预防、准备与响应

Part 1: ★ 预防体系概览（2分钟）
A: We mainly manage three emergency scenarios: fire, typhoon and on-site medical emergency. Do we have complete prevention systems for all of them?
A: 我们工厂主要管控三类应急场景：火灾、台风、现场医疗应急，这三项是否都建立了完整的预防体系？
B: Yes. We have specific prevention plans for fire, typhoon and medical emergency. Daily hazard identification and monitoring is implemented to avoid incident sources in advance.
B: 有的。我们针对火灾、台风、医疗应急分别制定了具体的预防方案，通过日常隐患排查和监控，事先从源头预防事故发生。
A: So prevention is the foundation of our entire emergency management system.
A: 所以预防是我们整个应急管理体系的基础。
Part 2: ★★ 火灾应急准备（2分钟）
A: What daily preparation work do we do for fire emergency?
A: 针对火灾应急，日常常态化准备工作包含哪些？
B: We inspect fire alarms, fire extinguishers, fire suppression system and evacuation signs regularly. Exit routes and assembly points are always kept clear and accessible.
B: 我们定期点检消防报警、灭火器材、灭火系统和疏散标识，确保逃生通道、集合点全程畅通可用。
A: How often do we check the fire extinguishers?
A: 灭火器多久检查一次？
B: We conduct regular inspections and ensure all fire extinguishers are in working condition and properly placed.
B: 我们定期点检，确保所有灭火器材处于正常工作状态并正确摆放。
Part 3: ★★ 台风与医疗应急（2分钟）
A: How do we prepare for typhoon risks every year?
A: 针对台风，我们每年的预防准备工作如何开展？
B: We track real-time weather alerts, fix outdoor materials that may fall down, arrange on-duty team members and personnel evacuation in advance.
B: 我们实时跟进气象预警，提前加固户外可能被吹落的物品，安排人员值班，做好人员事先避险安排。
A: What preparation measures do we have for on-site medical emergencies?
A: 针对员工突发伤病的医疗应急，有哪些常态化准备措施？
B: Full-site first aid kits are well stocked. We have emergency hospital contacts, arrange on-site first aiders on duty every day, and nurses on duty in the clinic.
B: 全厂急救药品器材齐全充足，有急救医院联络方式，每日配置现场急救人员和医务室护士值班。
Part 4: ★★★ 培训与响应（2分钟）
A: Do we provide systematic emergency training for all employees?
A: 我们是否对全体员工开展系统化的应急培训？
B: Yes. All staff receive training for fire evacuation, typhoon and basic first aid. New employees must complete these trainings included in new hire orientation prior to work.
B: 是的。全员开展火灾逃生、防台风避险、基础急救培训，新员工入职时必须接受这些培训后方可上岗。
A: Once an emergency happens, what is our standard emergency response procedure?
A: 一旦突发险情，我们的标准应急响应流程是什么？
B: We follow the process: report immediately, evacuate personnel, control on-site risks. For fire, on-duty staff put out initial fire, trigger alarms, organize full evacuation and assemble for roll call.
B: 我们执行流程：立即报告，人员疏散、控制现场风险。火灾时，值班人员快速扑救初期火灾、启动报警、组织全员疏散、集合点名。
A: And for employee injury?
A: 员工伤病怎么处理？
B: We provide on-site first aid immediately and send the employee to hospital in time.
B: 第一时间现场急救、及时送医救治。
Part 5: ★★★★ 评估改进（2分钟）
A: How often do we conduct emergency drills? What types?
A: 我们应急演练频次如何？固定开展哪些演练项目？
B: We conduct regular fire evacuation drills, typhoon emergency drills and medical rescue drills every year.
B: 我们每年常态化开展火灾疏散演练、台风应急演练、现场医疗救护演练。
A: Do we conduct evaluation after every drill or real incident?
A: 每次演练或真实事故结束后，是否都做专项评估？
B: We work out corrective actions with clear responsible persons and completion deadlines, and verify completion until a complete closure.
B: 针对问题制定纠正措施，明确责任人与完成期限，验收合格后方可闭环。
A: So we stick to full-cycle management: prevention first, sufficient preparation, standard response, regular drills and continuous improvement.
A: 所以我们坚持全周期管理：预防为先、充分准备、规范响应、常态演练、持续改进。
练习建议：按顺序练习 Part 1→ Part 2→ Part 3→ Part 4→ Part 5。每部分先慢速练习，再使用正常语速朗读。
