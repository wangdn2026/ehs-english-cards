整体管控框架Overall Control Framework
GM 总经理
I just heard that one employee slipped and nearly fell on the stair landing yesterday. I'm quite concerned about slip and fall risks in our factory. What's our overall control framework for this kind of hazard?
我刚听说昨天有员工在楼梯平台滑倒险些出事，我很担心厂区内滑倒跌倒的隐患，咱们针对这类风险整体管控方案是怎样的？
EHS Manager 安全主管
Sir, our whole control system covers three core parts: facility design standards, regular site inspections, standardized daily housekeeping management, and also behavioral safety promotion.
我们整套管控体系分为四大核心板块：设施硬件标准、常态化现场巡检、标准化日常保洁管理，还有员工行为安全宣传管控。

2
硬件改造Hardware Improvements
GM 总经理
For stairways, ramps and wet areas like pantries, what hardware improvements have we put in place to prevent slips?
针对楼梯、坡道还有茶水间这类潮湿区域，我们已经落地了哪些硬件改造来防滑？
EHS Manager 安全主管
All stairs and ramps are fitted with complete handrails and anti-slip surface materials, we added extra lighting for stairwells, and laid anti-slip flooring in all wet work areas.
所有楼梯、坡道都配齐了扶手与防滑面层，楼梯间增补了照明，全部潮湿作业区都铺设了防滑地面。
handrails扶手 anti-slip flooring防滑地面
3
定期巡检Regular Inspections
GM 总经理
How do we carry out regular inspections across indoor workshops, outdoor parking lots and building entrances?
室内车间、室外停车场、厂区大门这些区域，我们是怎么开展定期巡检的？
EHS Manager 安全主管
We arrange daily routine walkthroughs for indoor zones, weekly special checks for walking surfaces, and extra patrols for outdoor areas on rainy or snowy days. Inspectors will also remind staff who look at mobile phones while walking during rounds.
室内区域我们安排每日常规巡查，每周专门针对行走地面做专项排查，雨雪天气会给室外区域增加巡逻频次。巡检时我们也会当场提醒走路低头看手机的员工。
walkways通道
4
日常保洁与行为安全Daily Housekeeping & Behavioral Safety
GM 总经理
What daily cleaning rules do we have to avoid spills, scattered debris and blocked walkways? Also I remember we had falls caused by staff scrolling phones while walking before, how do we manage this risk?
我们有哪些日常保洁规定，用来杜绝地面积水、杂物散落、通道堵塞这些问题？另外我记得之前也发生过员工走路看手机摔倒的事故，这块风险我们是怎么管控的？
EHS Manager 安全主管
Any liquid spill must be fully cleaned up right away, all walkways must stay free of obstacles, loose cables and uneven floor tiles will be fixed timely. Meanwhile all departments hold regular communication meetings and post safety posters to warn everyone not to use phones when moving around the plant.
任何液体泼洒都必须立刻彻底清理，所有通道保持无障碍物，散乱线缆、起翘破损地砖都会及时修复。同时各部门定期开会宣导，厂区张贴安全海报，反复提醒员工行走途中不要使用手机。
liquid spill液体泼洒 safety posters安全海报
5
洞口围栏与整改流程Floor Openings & Rectification Process
GM 总经理
How do we handle floor holes, uneven ground and stair openings that may lead to falling accidents?
对于容易引发坠落事故的地面洞口、凹凸地面、楼梯缺口，我们是怎么处理的？
EHS Manager 安全主管
All floor openings are covered or surrounded by barricades with warning signs, uneven ground will be marked clearly until full repair.
所有地面洞口都会加盖或者用带警示标识的围栏围住，凹凸地面会贴好明显警示，直到完全修复平整。
GM 总经理
Once inspectors find any slip or trip hazard on site, what's our complete rectification process?
一旦巡检人员在现场发现滑倒、绊倒隐患，完整的整改流程是怎样的？
EHS Manager 安全主管
We will record the hazard, assign responsible staff with a time limit for correction, and recheck the spot afterwards to confirm risks are removed.
我们会登记隐患信息，指定责任人并设定整改期限，整改完成后复查现场，确认隐患彻底消除。
floor openings地面洞口 barricades围栏 rectification整改
