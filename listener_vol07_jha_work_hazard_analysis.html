新任务 JHA

Job Hazard Analysis on a New Job
GET YOU · AI试驾 出品 | 安全五点半
1
启动 JHAStart the JHA Process
Supervisor 主管
We have a new task coming up. What should we do first to make sure it's safe?
我们有一项新任务要开展，首先要怎么做才能确保安全？
Safety Officer 安全员
Before the start, you should complete a Job Hazard Analysis (JHA).
在开工前，必须先做工作危害分析（JHA）。
Supervisor 主管
Alright. How do we start the JHA process?
好的，那JHA流程要怎么开始？
Safety Officer 安全员
First, select the job and define the scope of work.
首先，选定这项工作，明确作业范围。
new task新任务 JHA工作危害分析 before the start开工前 scope of work作业范围
2
组建团队 分解任务Build JHA Team & Break Down the Job
Supervisor 主管
Then what? Do I need to involve others?
然后呢？需要其他人参与吗？
Safety Officer 安全员
Yes. Select the JHA team including a jobmaster, operators, and me.
是的，组建JHA小组，包括作业师傅、操作人员和我。
Supervisor 主管
Next step?
下一步呢？
Safety Officer 安全员
Break down the job into steps and analyze hazards for each step.
把工作分解成具体步骤，对每一步进行危害分析。
JHA teamJHA 小组 jobmaster作业师傅 operators操作人员 break down分解工作
3
识别危害 控制措施Analyze Hazards & Develop Controls
Supervisor 主管
After we finish the analysis?
分析完成之后呢？
Safety Officer 安全员
Develop control measures and determine the follow-up actions to implement them.
制定控制措施，并确定后续跟进行动去落实。
Supervisor 主管
Got it. We'll do JHA properly before the new job starts.
明白了，新任务开工前我们会认真做好JHA。
analyze hazards危害分析 control measures控制措施 follow-up actions后续跟进行动
