10分钟对话练习：演练评估与改进（消防疏散）

Part 1: ★ 演练目的与频次（2分钟）
A: How many fire evacuation drills are required for our factory every year?
A: 我们工厂每年需要开展几次消防逃生演习？
B: As a key fire prevention unit, we conduct two fire evacuation drills every year, one in the first half and one in the second half.
B: 我厂属消防重点单位，全年两次全员演练，上下半年各一次。
A: So the drill frequency is twice a year for all employees.
A: 所以演练频次是每年两次全员参与。
Part 2: ★★ 演练筹备（2分钟）
A: What preparation work will be made before the drill?
A: 演练正式开始前需要做哪些筹备工作？
B: I submit the drill plan to management for approval and arrange a pre-drill meeting with facility, security and department representatives.
B: 我向管理层报批演练方案，组织厂务、保安、各部门代表召开演练筹备会。
A: Any other pre-drill preparation items?
A: 还有其他前期准备事项吗？
B: We conduct refresher training to all employees about the evacuation plan and procedures, generally by sending emails.
B: 对全员开展疏散预案和流程的再培训，一般通过发邮件方式进行。
Part 3: ★★ ERT分工执行（2分钟）
A: How do you arrange ERT responsibilities during the on-site drill?
A: 演练实施阶段ERT人员分工如何设置？
B: We have three teams: evacuation guide team, floor sweeping team, and first aid team under the ERT (Emergency Response Team).
B: 设立三支应急小组：疏散引导组、逐层扫楼组、现场急救组，隶属应急响应小组。
A: Who takes attendance at the assembly point?
A: 人员抵达集合点后由谁负责点名？
B: Each department representative takes the roll call and reports to the E. H. S. department.
B: 各部门代表现场点名，汇总人数上报E. H. S.部门。
Part 4: ★★★ 疏散流程（2分钟）
A: Is there a timeline requirement for the whole building evacuation?
A: 整栋楼宇人员撤离有没有时限要求？
B: All team members shall evacuate to the assembly point within 3 minutes.
B: 全体人员必须在3分钟内全部撤离至室外指定集合点。
A: What happens when everyone reaches the assembly point?
A: 所有人到达集合点后会怎样？
B: Department representatives take the roll call immediately and report the headcount to E. H. S. to confirm all personnel are accounted for.
B: 各部门代表立即点名，汇总人数上报E. H. S.，确认所有人员安全到位。
A: So the flow is: alarm triggers, evacuate to assembly point, then roll call.
A: 所以流程是：报警触发、疏散到集合点、然后点名确认。
Part 5: ★★★★ 评估整改（2分钟）
A: What is the next step once the drill finishes?
A: 整场演练结束后首要工作是什么？
B: We organize an immediate post-drill debriefing meeting for overall performance review using our official drill evaluation scoring form.
B: 第一时间召开复盘会议，使用正式评分表对演练整体表现开展评审。
A: Could you specify the score weight of each segment?
A: 请说明各板块分值占比。
B: Preparation stage takes 25 points, on-site evacuation process 40 points, emergency response 25 points, and post-drill improvement closure 10 points.
B: 筹备阶段25分，现场疏散流程40分，现场应急处置25分，事后整改闭环10分。
A: How to deal with non-conformities identified from the drill?
A: 演练排查出的不足之处如何处置？
B: We list all findings and come up with corrective action plans. E. H. S. tracks progress regularly and verifies final completion.
B: 梳理所有问题点，制定整改方案。E. H. S.持续跟进整改进度并验证关闭。
A: So we update the evacuation plan to achieve continuous improvement.
A: 所以我们同步更新疏散预案，实现持续改进。
练习建议：按顺序练习 Part 1→ Part 2→ Part 3→ Part 4→ Part 5。每部分先慢速练习，再使用正常语速朗读。
