应急准备总框架

Emergency Preparedness Overview
安全五点半 · EHS职场英语
1
紧急情况识别Emergency Identification
GM 总经理
We are going to update our company's Emergency Preparedness and Response Plan. Can you explain the main framework of this EPRP plan?
我们要更新公司的应急准备与响应计划。你说明一下这份EPRP计划的主要框架是什么？
E. H. S. Manager 安全主管
Certainly. The whole plan starts with identification of all potential on-site emergencies. We select ten most common and high-frequency workplace emergencies for our site.
没问题。整个计划首先是识别现场所有潜在紧急情况。我们筛选了厂区最常见、高发的10类突发事件。
GM 总经理
Could you list the ten typical emergencies?
能列举一下这十种典型突发事件吗？
E. H. S. Manager 安全主管
Fire accident, on-site medical emergency, pandemic or contagious disease, foodborne illness, hazardous material spill or leak on site, confined space rescue, natural disasters such as earthquake, typhoon, heavy rain and lightning storm, unexpected power outage and electrical failure, personnel trapped accident, and equipment abnormal shutdown and machine failure.
火灾事故、现场医疗急救、大规模流行病或传染性疾病、食源性疾病、现场危险物质泄漏、有限空间救援、自然灾害如地震台风暴雨雷击、突发停电和电气故障、人员被困事故，以及设备异常停机和机械故障。

2
准备与响应Preparation & Response
GM 总经理
Understood. Besides emergency identification, what core components must be included in our EPRP?
了解。除了风险识别，我们的应急计划还必须包含哪些核心模块？
E. H. S. Manager 安全主管
The plan contains two major parts: emergency preparation and emergency response.
该方案包含两大板块：应急准备与应急处置。
GM 总经理
What exactly do we need to do for emergency preparation?
我们具体需要做哪些应急准备工作？
E. H. S. Manager 安全主管
For preparation, we need regular hazard identification like safety inspection, incident reporting and investigation, emergency equipment maintenance, safety training for all employees, documented evacuation routes and fixed assembly points.
筹备工作需开展常态化危险源辨识工作，包括安全检查、事故上报与调查、应急设备维保、全员安全培训，同时留存书面疏散路线文件并设置固定集合点。
GM 总经理
Very good. Then what about the emergency response procedures during an actual incident?
很好。那发生真实事故时的应急处置流程是怎样的？
E. H. S. Manager 安全主管
Once an emergency happens, we follow standard response steps: incident reporting, onsite isolation, risk control, personnel evacuation, first aid treatment, and site lockdown if needed.
一旦发生突发事件，我们将遵循标准处置流程：事件上报、现场隔离、风险管控、人员疏散、紧急救治，必要时封锁事发区域。
hazard identification危险源辨识 evacuation route疏散路线 assembly point集合点
3
职责与演练Responsibilities & Drills
GM 总经理
Does the plan clarify roles and responsibilities for each department?
该方案是否明确了各部门的岗位职责？
E. H. S. Manager 安全主管
Yes. We define clear EHS responsibilities for production, admin, maintenance, HR, and management teams. Each department has its own emergency response responsibilities.
是的。我们为生产、行政、维修、人力资源及管理团队明确划分环境、健康与安全职责，各部门均承担对应的应急处置职责。
GM 总经理
What about drills and follow-up actions?
演练与后续跟进措施该如何安排？
E. H. S. Manager 安全主管
We schedule regular emergency drills, record all drill results, identify gaps, implement corrective actions, and update the EPRP plan annually.
我们定期组织应急演练，记录所有演练结果，排查存在的不足，落实整改措施，并每年更新应急准备响应计划。
emergency response responsibilitiesEHS应急处置职责 corrective action整改措施

完整讨论Full Discussion

Do we have any other key considerations for this plan?
针对这份方案，我们还有其他需要重点考量的事项吗？

Yes. We also cover emergency communication channels, emergency contact lists, rescue resources allocation, and post-incident review and recovery procedures.
是的。我们还涵盖应急通信渠道、紧急联系人清单、救援资源调配以及事后复盘与恢复流程。

Perfect. This complete framework meets our corporate EHS audit requirements. Please finalize the formal EPRP document accordingly.

E. H. S. Manager 安全主管
Sure, I will complete and submit the updated Emergency Preparedness and Response Plan soon.
好的，我会尽快完成并提交更新后的应急准备与响应方案。
