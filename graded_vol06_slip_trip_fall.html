10分钟对话练习：行走区域防滑防跌倒

Part 1: ★ 风险识别（2分钟）
A: Did you hear about the slip incident on the stair landing yesterday?
A: 你听说昨天楼梯平台的滑倒事件了吗？
B: Yes, I'm quite concerned about slip and fall risks in our factory. What's our overall control framework?
B: 听说了，我很担心厂区内滑倒跌倒的隐患。咱们的整体管控方案是怎样的？
A: Our control system covers four core parts: facility design standards, regular site inspections, standardized daily housekeeping, and behavioral safety promotion.
A: 我们的管控体系涵盖四大核心板块：设施硬件标准、常态化现场巡检、标准化日常保洁，还有行为安全宣传。
B: Which areas are at the highest risk?
B: 哪些区域风险最高？
A: Stair landings, ramps, wet areas like pantries, and outdoor zones during rainy weather.
A: 楼梯平台、坡道、茶水间等潮湿区域，以及雨雪天气的室外区域。
Part 2: ★ 防滑硬件设施（2分钟）
B: What hardware improvements have we put in place for stairs and ramps?
B: 楼梯和坡道做了哪些硬件改造？
A: All stairs and ramps are fitted with complete handrails and anti-slip surface materials.
A: 所有楼梯和坡道都配齐了扶手与防滑面层。
B: What about wet areas like pantries?
B: 茶水间等潮湿区域呢？
A: We've added extra lighting for stairwells and laid anti-slip flooring in all wet work areas.
A: 楼梯间增补了照明，全部潮湿作业区都铺设了防滑地面。
B: How about outdoor parking lots and building entrances?
B: 室外停车场和厂区大门呢？
A: Those areas receive extra patrols on rainy or snowy days to ensure safe walking conditions.
A: 这些区域在雨雪天气会增加巡逻频次，确保行走安全。
Part 3: ★★ 巡检与保洁（2分钟）
B: How do we carry out regular inspections across different areas?
B: 我们怎么在不同区域开展定期巡检？
A: We arrange daily routine walkthroughs for indoor zones and weekly special checks for walking surfaces.
A: 室内区域安排每日常规巡查，每周专门针对行走地面做专项排查。
B: What daily cleaning rules do we have?
B: 日常保洁有哪些规定？
A: Any liquid spill must be fully cleaned up right away, and all walkways must stay free of obstacles.
A: 任何液体泼洒都必须立刻彻底清理，所有通道保持无障碍物。
B: What about loose cables and damaged floor tiles?
B: 散乱线缆和破损地砖怎么处理？
A: They will be fixed timely to prevent trip hazards.
A: 会及时修复，防止绊倒隐患。
Part 4: ★★★ 洞口管控与行为安全（2分钟）
B: How do we handle floor openings and stair openings that may lead to falling accidents?
B: 地面洞口和楼梯缺口这些可能导致坠落的隐患怎么处理？
A: All floor openings are covered or surrounded by barricades with warning signs.
A: 所有地面洞口都会加盖或者用带警示标识的围栏围住。
B: What about uneven ground?
B: 凹凸不平的地面呢？
A: Uneven ground will be marked clearly until full repair is completed.
A: 凹凸地面会贴好明显警示，直到完全修复平整。
B: I remember we had falls caused by staff scrolling phones while walking. How do we manage this?
B: 我记得之前发生过员工走路看手机摔倒的事故，这块怎么管控？
A: All departments hold regular communication meetings and post safety posters to warn everyone not to use phones when moving around the plant.
A: 各部门定期开会宣导，厂区张贴安全海报，反复提醒员工行走途中不要使用手机。
Part 5: ★★★★ 整改流程（2分钟）
B: Once inspectors find any slip or trip hazard on site, what's the complete rectification process?
B: 巡检人员在现场发现滑倒、绊倒隐患后，完整的整改流程是怎样的？
A: We will record the hazard, assign responsible staff with a time limit for correction.
A: 我们会登记隐患信息，指定责任人并设定整改期限。
B: And then?
B: 然后呢？
A: We recheck the spot afterwards to confirm risks are completely removed.
A: 整改完成后复查现场，确认隐患彻底消除。
B: So it's a closed-loop process: identify, record, correct, and verify.
B: 所以是一个闭环流程：识别、登记、整改、验证。
A: Exactly. This systematic approach ensures no hazard is left unaddressed.
A: 没错，这种系统化方法确保每一个隐患都不会被遗漏。
练习建议：按顺序练习 Part 1→ Part 2→ Part 3→ Part 4→ Part 5。每部分先慢速练习，再使用正常语速朗读。
