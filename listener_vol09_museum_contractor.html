承包商PTP 作业前方案Contractor PTP Planning
PM 项目经理
Our contractors have finished all pre-qualification and pre-job preparations and will start on-site work very soon. What safety management process do they need to complete before they begin work?
我们的承包商已经完成所有资格预审和入场准备，马上就要进场作业。开工前他们需要完成哪些安全管理工作？
EHS Manager EHS经理
Before any contractor task starts, they should complete an overall pre-task hazard identification and risk assessment for every upcoming work activity. This is the foundation of all on-site risk control.
在承包商任何作业开始前，他们必须针对即将开展的所有工作，完成整体的危害辨识和风险评估，这是所有现场风险管控的基础。
PM 项目经理
After they finish hazard identification and risk evaluation, what do they need to do next?
完成危害辨识和风险评估之后，下一步要做什么？
EHS Manager EHS经理
They need to fill out a Pre-Task Plan (PTP) and get approvals. PTP is also referred to as Work Permit in many companies. This document records the related risks, control measures and other safety requirements for the task.
他们需要填写并审批作业前方案，也就是很多公司说的作业许可证。这份文件会记录作业相关的风险、管控措施和安全要求。
PM 项目经理
Besides risk analysis and permit approval, what on-site safety work do they need to conduct?
除了风险分析和许可审批，开工后我们还要落实哪些现场安全工作？
EHS Manager EHS经理
They need to ensure all the safety measures indicated in the PTP are in place before the start of the work. In addition, they should perform routine on-site safety inspections at least every week to check contractor workers' behaviors, PPE compliance and risk control status.
他们需要确保作业开始前，作业方案（PTP）中列明的所有安全措施均落实到位。此外，他们至少每周要开展现场安全巡查，核查承包商作业行为、防护用品佩戴以及风险管控的情况。
Pre-Task Plan (PTP)作业前方案 hazard identification危险源辨识 on-site safety inspection现场安全巡查

安全会议与奖惩Safety Meetings & Rewards
PM 项目经理
Do they have regular safety meetings for ongoing contractor projects?
针对承包商持续作业的项目，他们有定期安全会议吗？
EHS Manager EHS经理
Yes. They hold safety meetings at least on a weekly basis. We review current safety performance, existing hazards and closure status of the corrective actions.
有的。承包商每周至少要开一次安全会议，复盘审核当前安全表现、现存隐患和整改进度。
PM 项目经理
Do they have reward and penalty rules for the contractors on site?
他们针对现场承包商，有没有一套奖惩规则？
EHS Manager EHS经理
Yes. They issue fines for any safety violation by contractor workers. At the same time, anyone who proactively reports unsafe acts, unsafe conditions or offers good safety suggestions will receive a safety reward.
有的。如果查到安全违规，他们会对承包商工人进行罚款；同时，对主动上报不安全行为、隐患或提出有效安全建议的，会给予安全奖励。
PM 项目经理
To summarize, the whole process covers pre-task hazard identification and risk assessment, PTP application and approval, ongoing site inspection, weekly safety review meetings, and a penalty and reward program. Correct?
总结来说，整套流程包括：作业前危害辨识和风险评估、PTP 作业许可申请审批、持续现场巡查、每周安全复盘会议，以及奖惩制度。对吗？
EHS Manager EHS经理
Yes. This is the complete and standard contractor safety management process for our site.
没错，这就是我们厂区标准、完整的承包商安全管理流程。
safety meeting安全会议 safety violation安全违规 safety reward安全奖励

承包商分类与总体要求Contractor Classification
PM 项目经理
We will start a facility renovation project soon. What safety work should we arrange for contractors in advance?
我们马上要启动厂务设施改造项目，针对承包商，我们前期要做好哪些安全工作？
EHS Manager EHS经理
Contractor safety management should be included in the whole project. We classify contractors into three categories: office-based team members, facilities support services, and renovation or construction contractors.
承包商安全管理要融入项目全程。我们将承包商分为三类：驻办公室团队服务、厂务后勤服务，以及装修、设备维保与施工工程。
PM 项目经理
Are there different safety requirements for these three types?
这三类承包商的安全要求各不相同吗？
EHS Manager EHS经理
Yes. They have different requirements for pre-qualifications, trainings, signing of a contract, safety meeting, PTP, worksite inspection, annual audit and quarterly performance review.
是的，他们在资格预审、培训、合同签署、安全会议、作业前方案/作业许可、现场检查、年度审核及季度绩效评审方面有着不同要求。
office-based team驻办公室团队 facilities support厂务后勤

资格预审与筛选Pre-Qualification & Selection
PM 项目经理
What is the core task of the first stage, Pre-Qualification and Selection?
第一阶段资格预审与筛选，核心工作是什么？
EHS Manager EHS经理
We must collect a completed Contractor Prequalification Form from every bidding contractor at first.
我们首先必须收取所有投标承包商填写完整的《承包商资格预审表》。
PM 项目经理
What key contents do we need to check on this pre-qualification form?
这份资格预审表，我们重点要核查哪些内容？
EHS Manager EHS经理
We check their licences, certifications, qualifications, three-year reportable injuries, safety citations from Gov. agencies and some items of their E. H. S. management systems.
我们核查其执照、资质证书、从业资格、近三年可记录工伤事故、政府监管机构出具的安全违规处罚通知以及一些EHS管理系统方面的问题。
PM 项目经理
Once we select a qualified contractor after pre-qualification, what is the next key step?
我们通过预审选定合格承包商之后，下一步关键流程是什么？
EHS Manager EHS经理
All contractor staff must finish our designated online orientation training and their specific trainings.
所有承包商人员必须完成我们指定的线上入职安全培训，还有他们自己的专项培训。
Prequalification Form资格预审表 safety citations安全违规通知 orientation training入职培训
