10分钟对话练习：应急准备总框架

Part 1: ★ 紧急情况识别（2分钟）
A: We are going to update our company's Emergency Preparedness and Response Plan. Can you explain the main framework?
A: 我们要更新公司的应急准备与响应计划。你说明一下主要框架是什么？
B: The plan starts with identification of all potential emergencies on site. We selected ten most common and high-frequency typical workplace emergencies.
B: 整个计划首先是识别现场所有潜在紧急情况。我们筛选了厂区最常见、高发的10类突发事件。
A: Could you list them?
A: 能列举一下吗？
B: Fire accident, on-site medical emergency, pandemic or contagious disease, foodborne illness, hazardous material spill, confined space rescue, natural disasters, power outage, personnel trapped accident, and equipment abnormal shutdown.
B: 火灾事故、现场医疗急救、流行病或传染性疾病、食源性疾病、危险物质泄漏、有限空间救援、自然灾害、突发停电、人员被困事故，以及设备异常停机。
Part 2: ★★ 计划框架（2分钟）
A: Besides emergency identification, what core components must be included in our EPRP?
A: 除了风险识别，应急计划还必须包含哪些核心模块？
B: The plan contains two major parts: emergency preparation and emergency response.
B: 该方案包含两大板块：应急准备与应急处置。
A: What exactly do we need to do for emergency preparation?
A: 具体需要做哪些应急准备工作？
B: We need regular hazard identification, safety inspection, incident reporting and investigation, emergency equipment maintenance, and safety training for all employees.
B: 需开展常态化危险源辨识，包括安全检查、事故上报与调查、应急设备维保、全员安全培训。
Part 3: ★★ 准备工作（2分钟）
A: What specific preparations do we need for evacuation?
A: 疏散方面需要哪些具体准备？
B: We must maintain documented evacuation routes and fixed assembly points. All routes must stay clear and accessible at all times.
B: 必须留存书面疏散路线文件并设置固定集合点。所有路线必须始终保持畅通。
A: And for emergency equipment?
A: 应急设备方面呢？
B: Regular maintenance of all emergency equipment is required, including fire extinguishers, first aid kits, and communication devices.
B: 所有应急设备需定期维保，包括灭火器、急救器材和通信设备。
A: So hazard identification and evacuation planning are the foundation of our preparation work.
A: 所以危险源辨识和疏散规划是我们准备工作的基础。
Part 4: ★★★ 应急响应（2分钟）
A: What is our standard response procedure during an actual emergency?
A: 发生真实事故时的标准处置流程是怎样的？
B: Once an emergency happens, we follow standard steps: incident reporting, onsite isolation, risk control, personnel evacuation, first aid treatment, and site lockdown if needed.
B: 一旦发生突发事件，遵循标准处置流程：事件上报、现场隔离、风险管控、人员疏散、紧急救治，必要时封锁事发区域。
A: How do we handle emergency communication during the response?
A: 响应过程中如何处理应急通信？
B: We use established emergency communication channels and contact lists to coordinate rescue resources and notify all relevant personnel.
B: 我们使用既定的应急通信渠道和联系人清单来协调救援资源并通知所有相关人员。
A: So the response is a systematic process from reporting to recovery.
A: 所以响应是一个从上报到恢复的系统化流程。
Part 5: ★★★★ 职责与演练（2分钟）
A: Does the plan clarify roles and responsibilities for each department?
A: 方案是否明确了各部门的岗位职责？
B: Yes. We define clear E. H. S. responsibilities for production, admin, maintenance, HR, and management teams. Each department has its own emergency response duties.
B: 是的。我们为生产、行政、维修、人力资源及管理团队明确划分E. H. S.职责，各部门均承担对应的应急处置职责。
A: What about drills and follow-up actions?
A: 演练与后续跟进措施该如何安排？
B: We schedule regular emergency drills, record all results, identify gaps, and implement corrective actions.
B: 我们定期组织应急演练，记录所有结果，排查不足，落实整改措施。
A: And we update the plan annually?
A: 并且每年更新计划？
B: Exactly. We update the EPRP annually and cover emergency communication channels, contact lists, rescue resources, and post-incident review procedures.
B: 没错。每年更新应急准备响应计划，涵盖应急通信渠道、联系人清单、救援资源及事后复盘与恢复流程。
练习建议：按顺序练习 Part 1→ Part 2→ Part 3→ Part 4→ Part 5。每部分先慢速练习，再使用正常语速朗读。
