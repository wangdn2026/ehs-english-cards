前台咨询与挂号Front Desk & Registration

Excuse me, where is the internal medicine department for fever and cold symptoms? Which floor and building is it on?
打扰一下，请问看感冒发烧的内科门诊在哪一栋楼、几楼？
Receptionist 导诊员
Internal medicine is on the 2nd floor of Building A. You can register your appointment right here first.
内科在A栋二楼，你可以先在我这里挂号。
Patient 病人
Alright, here is my medical insurance card.
好的，这是我的医保卡。
Receptionist 导诊员
Wait for your number on the screen. The doctor will call you when it's your turn.
在屏幕上等候叫号就可以，轮到你时医生会喊你的名字。

2
问诊医生：症状与诊断Doctor Consultation
Patient 病人
I've had a bad cold for three days, with a high fever, sore throat and constant coughing.
我重感冒三天了，发高烧、喉咙疼，还一直咳嗽。
Doctor 医生
Let me check your temperature, pulse and breathing first. Have you had any chronic illness before?
我先帮你量体温、检查脉搏和呼吸。你以前有慢性病吗？
Patient 病人
No, I've always been pretty healthy.
没有，我身体一直都挺好的。
Doctor 医生
It's just a viral cold, no serious infection. I'll prescribe some fever-reducer and cough syrup for you.
只是病毒性感冒，没有严重感染。我给你开退烧药和止咳糖浆。
chronic illness慢性病 viral cold病毒性感冒 follow-up check-up定期复查
3
承包商分类与总体要求Contractor Classification
PM 项目经理
We will start a facility renovation project soon. What safety work should we arrange for contractors in advance?
我们马上要启动厂务设施改造项目，针对承包商，我们前期要做好哪些安全工作？
EHS Manager EHS经理
Contractor safety management should be included in the whole project. We classify contractors into three categories: office-based team members, facilities support services, and renovation or construction contractors.
承包商安全管理要融入项目全程。我们将承包商分为三类：驻办公室团队服务、厂务后勤服务，以及装修、设备维保与施工工程。
PM 项目经理
Are there different safety requirements for these three types?
这三类承包商的安全要求各不相同吗？
EHS Manager EHS经理
Yes. They have different requirements for pre-qualifications, trainings, signing of a contract, safety meeting, PTP, worksite inspection, annual audit and quarterly performance review.
是的，他们在资格预审、培训、合同签署、安全会议、作业前方案/作业许可、现场检查、年度审核及季度绩效评审方面有着不同要求。
office-based team驻办公室团队 facilities support厂务后勤
4
资格预审与筛选Pre-Qualification & Selection
PM 项目经理
What is the core task of the first stage, Pre-Qualification and Selection?
第一阶段资格预审与筛选，核心工作是什么？
EHS Manager EHS经理
We must collect a completed Contractor Prequalification Form from every bidding contractor at first.
我们首先必须收取所有投标承包商填写完整的《承包商资格预审表》。
PM 项目经理
What key contents do we need to check on this pre-qualification form?
这份资格预审表，我们重点要核查哪些内容？
EHS Manager EHS经理
We check their licences, certifications, qualifications, three-year reportable injuries, safety citations from Gov. agencies and some items of their E. H. S. management systems.
我们核查其执照、资质证书、从业资格、近三年可记录工伤事故、政府监管机构出具的安全违规处罚通知以及一些EHS管理系统方面的问题。
PM 项目经理
Once we select a qualified contractor after pre-qualification, what is the next key step?
我们通过预审选定合格承包商之后，下一步关键流程是什么？
EHS Manager EHS经理
All contractor staff must finish our designated online orientation training and their specific trainings.
所有承包商人员必须完成我们指定的线上入职安全培训，还有他们自己的专项培训。
Prequalification Form资格预审表 safety citations安全违规通知 orientation training入职培训
5
入场安全准备Pre-Job Safety Preparation
PM 项目经理
Great, training is very important. Anything else?
很好，培训十分重要，还有其他事项吗？
EHS Manager EHS经理
Sure, they need to conduct hazard identification and risk assessment in advance for the contractor activities and submit their work safety plan.
当然，他们事先需针对承包商作业开展危险源辨识与风险评估，并提交作业安全方案。
PM 项目经理
Does the second stage require them to sign a safety agreement?
第二阶段作业前安全准备，要求签订安全协议？
EHS Manager EHS经理
Absolutely, we shall sign a formal Safety Agreement with them prior to the start of work in order to comply with Work Safety Law.
一定的，为了符合安全生产法的要求，在开工前和对方签订正式安全生产管理协议。
PM 项目经理
Got it. Safety is the foundation of every project.
明白了。安全是每一个项目的基础。
hazard identification危险源辨识 work safety plan作业安全方案 safety agreement安全协议
