10分钟对话练习：医院门诊与承包商安全管理
← 返回星级目录  |  显示全部
Part 1: ★ 前台咨询与挂号（2分钟）
Patient: Excuse me, where is the internal medicine department for fever and cold symptoms? Which floor and building is it on?
病人: 打扰一下，请问看感冒发烧的内科门诊在哪一栋楼、几楼？
Receptionist: Internal medicine is on the 2nd floor of Building A. You can register your appointment right here first.
导诊员: 内科在A栋二楼，你可以先在我这里挂号。
Patient: Alright, here is my medical insurance card.
病人: 好的，这是我的医保卡。
Receptionist: Wait for your number on the screen. The doctor will call you when it's your turn.
导诊员: 在屏幕上等候叫号就可以，轮到你时医生会喊你的名字。
Part 2: ★★ 问诊医生：症状与诊断（2分钟）
Doctor: Please take a seat. What brings you in today?
医生: 请坐。你今天是哪里不舒服？
Patient: I've had a bad cold for three days, with a high fever, sore throat and constant coughing.
病人: 我重感冒三天了，发高烧、喉咙疼，还一直咳嗽。
Doctor: Let me check your temperature, pulse and breathing first. Have you had any chronic illness before?
医生: 我先帮你量体温、检查脉搏和呼吸。你以前有慢性病吗？
Patient: No, I've always been pretty healthy.
病人: 没有，我身体一直都挺好的。
Doctor: It's just a viral cold, no serious infection. I'll prescribe some fever-reducer and cough syrup for you.
医生: 只是病毒性感冒，没有严重感染。我给你开退烧药和止咳糖浆。
Patient: Do I need to come back for a follow-up check-up?
病人: 我需要回来定期复查吗？
Part 3: ★★ 承包商分类与总体要求（2分钟）
PM: We will start a facility renovation project soon. What safety work should we arrange for contractors in advance?
项目经理: 我们马上要启动厂务设施改造项目，针对承包商，我们前期要做好哪些安全工作？
EHS Manager: Contractor safety management should be included in the whole project. We classify contractors into three categories: office-based team members, facilities support services, and renovation or construction contractors.
EHS经理: 承包商安全管理要融入项目全程。我们将承包商分为三类：驻办公室团队服务、厂务后勤服务，以及装修、设备维保与施工工程。
PM: Are there different safety requirements for these three types?
项目经理: 这三类承包商的安全要求各不相同吗？
EHS Manager: Yes. They have different requirements for pre-qualifications, trainings, signing of a contract, safety meeting, PTP, worksite inspection, annual audit and quarterly performance review.
EHS经理: 是的，他们在资格预审、培训、合同签署、安全会议、作业前方案/作业许可、现场检查、年度审核及季度绩效评审方面有着不同要求。
Part 4: ★★★ 资格预审与筛选（2分钟）
PM: What is the core task of the first stage, Pre-Qualification & Selection?
项目经理: 第一阶段资格预审与筛选，核心工作是什么？
EHS Manager: We must collect a completed Contractor Prequalification Form from every bidding contractor at first.
EHS经理: 我们首先必须收取所有投标承包商填写完整的《承包商资格预审表》。
PM: What key contents do we need to check on this pre-qualification form?
项目经理: 这份资格预审表，我们重点要核查哪些内容？
EHS Manager: We check their licences, certifications, qualifications, 3-year reportable injuries, safety citations from Gov. agencies and some items of their EHS management systems.
EHS经理: 我们核查其执照、资质证书、从业资格、近三年可记录工伤事故、政府监管机构出具的安全违规处罚通知以及一些EHS管理系统方面的问题。
PM: Once we select a qualified contractor after pre-qualification, what is the next key step?
项目经理: 我们通过预审选定合格承包商之后，下一步关键流程是什么？
EHS Manager: All contractor staff must finish our designated online orientation training and their specific trainings.
EHS经理: 所有承包商人员必须完成我们指定的线上入职安全培训，还有他们自己的专项培训。
Part 5: ★★★★ 入场安全准备（2分钟）
PM: Great, training is very important. Anything else?
项目经理: 很好，培训十分重要，还有其他事项吗？
EHS Manager: Sure, they need to conduct hazard identification and risk assessment in advance for the contractor activities and submit their work safety plan.
EHS经理: 当然，他们事先需针对承包商作业开展危险源辨识与风险评估，并提交作业安全方案。
PM: Does the second stage require them to sign a safety agreement?
项目经理: 第二阶段作业前安全准备，要求签订安全协议？
EHS Manager: Absolutely, we shall sign a formal Safety Agreement with them prior to the start of work in order to comply with Work Safety Law.
EHS经理: 一定的，为了符合安全生产法的要求，在开工前和对方签订正式安全生产管理协议。
PM: Got it. Safety is the foundation of every project.
项目经理: 明白了。安全是每一个项目的基础。
练习建议：按顺序练习 Part 1→ Part 2→ Part 3→ Part 4→ Part 5。每部分先慢速练习，再使用正常语速朗读。
